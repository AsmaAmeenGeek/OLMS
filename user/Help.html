Help & Support

About Us

OLMS by our Million Library is a modern platform offering users a seamless library management system for borrowing, returning, and managing books. Our mission is to make learning resources easily accessible to everyone. We’re not just a library; we’re a community of book lovers, and we want you to be a part of it! With features that allow users to send requests and receive messages from admins, we’re here to ensure that your library experience is smooth and enjoyable.



Plans

We offer a variety of subscription plans tailored to meet the needs of students, professionals, and book enthusiasts. Contact us to learn more about our competitive pricing and benefits.



Terms and Conditions

By using our services, you agree to abide by our terms and conditions. These include returning borrowed books on time, respecting library property, and adhering to our code of conduct. Failure to comply may result in account suspension.



FAQs

Find answers to commonly asked questions about our services, account setup, and borrowing process in our FAQ section.

1. How do I borrow a book?

To borrow a book, go to the 'All Books' section, search for the book you want, and click on the 'Borrow' button.

2. How do I check my borrowed books?

You can view all currently issued books in the 'Currently Issued Books' section.

3. How do I return a book?

Visit the 'Currently Issued Books' section and select the 'Return' button for the specific book.
